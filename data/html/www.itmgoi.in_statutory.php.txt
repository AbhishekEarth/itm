AICTE QIP PG Certificate Program
 In the academic year 2024-25, AICTE has introduced an 18-credit curriculum, spanning six
months, under the AICTE-QIP-PG Certificate Program in 'Emerging Areas.' This program is
designed for faculty members in core engineering disciplines, including Chemical,
Mechanical, Civil, and Electrical and Electronics Engineering. This certificate program
covers emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML),
Block-chain, Semi-conductors, and other advanced fields. Its objectives are to equip faculty
members with expertise in these cutting-edge technologies and to cultivate a pool of
academic professionals proficient in these areas.

In the first batch of the AICTE-QIP-PG Certificate Program, the following six faculty
members from our institute have successfully completed the certificate course.

1. Dr Shiv Kumar Sharma, Department of Mechanical Engineering
2. Mr. Amit Jain, Department of Mechanical Engineering
3. Mr. Amit Tiwari, Department of Mechanical Engineering
4. Dr. Ashutosh Shankar Trivedi, Department of Civil Engineering
5. Ms. Vishakha Yadav, Department of Civil Engineering
6. Dr. Sushant Jain, Department of Electronics & Communication Engineering
