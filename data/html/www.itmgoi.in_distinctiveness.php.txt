Harmony in Stone: Sculptural Collection
Our campus is adorned with the Harmony in Stone sculptures, crafted by renowned artists. Each piece reflects Indian culture and heritage, inspiring students to think critically and connect emotionally. The International Sculpture Symposium (2006) marked the beginning of this journey, bringing global artists and scholars together to celebrate the power of sculpture.

Harmony in Color: Painting Collection

Ibarat: Celebrating Words and Art
Ibarat, our annual literary and cultural event, brings together writers, poets, and artists. Since 2007, it has been a platform for intellectual exchange, enhancing students' critical thinking and communication skills through workshops, readings, and discussions.

Megh Malhar: A Musical Legacy 
Megh Malhar, an annual event since 2007, honors the legendary raag attributed to Tansen. It brings together students and music enthusiasts to explore the emotional and cultural depth of this iconic raag, reinforcing ITM’s commitment to artistic expression.
