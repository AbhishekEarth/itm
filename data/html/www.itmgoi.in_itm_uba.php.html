Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) Cell - ITM Gwalior


About UBA

Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) is a flagship program initiated by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, aimed at connecting higher educational institutions with rural communities to address development challenges. ITM Gwalior is proud to be an active participant in this transformative initiative, contributing to the betterment of society through its engagement with local villages.


Objective

  • To leverage the knowledge base of ITM Gwalior for addressing the rural challenges.
  • To foster a deeper engagement between students, faculty, and the rural community.
  • To promote sustainable rural development through innovative solutions.
  • To bridge the gap between urban and rural India by implementing need-based interventions.
  • To collaborate with sports organizations for enhanced training and participation opportunities.

UBA Activities

1.Village Adoption & Surveys – ITM Gwalior has adopted 5 villages under UBA and conducted extensive baseline surveys to identify key issues such as sanitation, education, employment, and healthcare.

Adopted Villages: Sarnagat, Badera, Belgada, Bargava, and Chetupada (Block: Dabra, District: Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh). ITM Gwalior has been a participating institute in Unnat Bharat Abhiyan since the 2018-19 session and continues to contribute actively.

2.Community Development Initiatives

  • Health & Hygiene Awareness: Organizing medical camps, menstrual hygiene awareness drives, and Swachh Bharat campaigns, ensuring accessibility to basic health facilities.
  • Educational Support: Providing teaching assistance, career guidance, and digital learning resources to rural students to improve literacy rates and career prospects.
  • School Library Establishment: Setting up libraries in government schools in adopted villages to enhance access to learning resources for students.
  • Increase in Student Enrollment: Due to UBA interventions, the number of student registrations in government schools in these villages has significantly improved.
  • Renewable Energy Promotion: Encouraging the use of solar energy, biogas, and sustainable farming practices to enhance energy efficiency and agricultural productivity.
  • Water Conservation Initiatives: Implementing rainwater harvesting systems in government building through the Local Administration on the basis of ITM UBA Cell initiative, water management awareness campaigns, and promoting sustainable irrigation techniques.
  • Waste Management & Environmental Protection: Promoting waste segregation, composting, and tree plantation drives to maintain ecological balance and reduce pollution in rural areas.
  • Infrastructure & Basic Needs Development: The UBA Cell has shared important reports regarding infrastructure gaps and basic needs with the Administration of Dabra and Gwalior, leading to action taken on various initiatives such as road repairs, sanitation improvements, and electrification in rural areas
  • Regular Village Visits & Activities: Every semester, 6-7 planned visits are conducted to UBA-adopted villages, where various community development activities are organized to ensure continued engagement and impact.

Achievements & Recognitions

  • Perennial Fund Award: ITM UBA Cell received recognition from IIT Delhi, the National Coordinating Institute of UBA, for its impactful contribution in adopted villages.
  • Reports & Interventions: The institution has submitted reports highlighting key rural issues and proposed solutions to the District Collector for necessary actions. Several of these recommendations have been implemented successfully based on ITM UBA Cell initiatives.
  • Recognition from Local Authorities: The Local Panchayat and Government Schools in adopted villages have appreciated ITM UBA Cell for its outstanding support and active participation in community development initiatives under Unnat Bharat Abhiyan.

Impact & Outcomes

  • Improved Literacy Rates: Enhanced access to education through school libraries and digital learning resources, leading to increased student enrollments in government schools.
  • Employment Generation: Vocational training programs have empowered rural youth and women with new skills, resulting in better job opportunities and self-employment.
  • Better Health & Hygiene: Increased awareness about sanitation, menstrual hygiene, and medical checkups, leading to improved health conditions in the villages.
  • Enhanced Rural Infrastructure: Collaboration with local administration has led to the development of roads, sanitation facilities, and electrification in adopted villages.
  • Environmental Sustainability: Successful implementation of waste management strategies, tree plantation drives, and solar energy adoption, promoting a cleaner and greener environment.
  • Community Empowerment: Strengthened participation of local communities in decision-making processes, ensuring long-term sustainable development.

How to Get Involved?

  • Students & Faculty: Join as volunteers and contribute through field visits, research, and project implementation.
  • Community Participation: : We encourage active involvement from local leaders and villagers to ensure effective problem-solving.
  • Collaborations & Partnerships: Organizations looking to collaborate for rural development projects are welcome.

Appreciation Letters under Unnat Bharat Abhiyan Activities.



ECO CLUB

Eco Club promotes environmental sustainability, raise awareness about ecological issues, and encourage students and faculty to engage in activities that contribute to a greener and more sustainable campus and community

LITERARY CLUB

Literary Club revolves around fostering creativity, critical thinking, and communication skills among students through a variety of literary activities.

SCIENCE AND TECHNOLOGY CLUB

Science and Technology Club fosters a deeper understanding of science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) among students, while promoting innovation, creativity, and hands-on learning. The club aims to achieve this by providing opportunities for students to explore new technologies, collaborate on projects, and stay updated on the latest advancements in their fields.

SPORTS CLUB

Sports Club typically aims to promoting physical fitness, team spirit, and overall well-being among students.

LIBRARY CLUB

Library club encourages students and faculty to utilize library resources regularly, fostering a habit of reading and research. The club hosts quiz competitions, debates, or book discussions related to technical and non-technical topics, fostering intellectual growth and curiosity.

RED RIBBON CLUB

Red Ribbon Club is a movement started by the Government of India in schools and colleges through which, students spread awareness over HIV / AIDS. The red ribbon is a symbol for both drug prevention and the fight against AIDS.

I’VENT’O STUDENTS CLUB

This club is a dynamic platform designed to empower students and elevate their skills across various domains, including technical, non-technical, and eSports. By organizing engaging competitions, hands-on workshops, and live technical projects, I’vent’o provides a unique environment for students to grow, collaborate, and showcase their talents.

GOOGLE DEVELOPER STUDENT CLUB (GDSC):

GDSC envisions empowering students through collaboration, innovation, and impactful solutions, fostering future tech leaders equipped to solve real-world challenges with cutting-edge technologies.

Electoral Literacy Club (ELC):

The Electoral Literacy Club (ELC) at ITM Gwalior is a student-driven initiative established to promote voter awareness, electoral participation, and democratic values among students. The club functions under the guidance of the Election Commission of India (ECI) aims to empower young voters by fostering electoral literacy and civic responsibility.
