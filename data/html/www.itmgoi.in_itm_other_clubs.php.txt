ECO CLUB
Eco Club promotes environmental sustainability, raise awareness about ecological issues, and encourage students and faculty to engage in activities that contribute to a greener and more sustainable campus and community                                        
                                            
Défi ECO 3.0 – Challenge 2025
The Eco Club, Research Cell, NSS UNIT ITM, in collaboration with the Department of Civil Engineering and NanoTech Foundation, Gwalior, presents Défi ECO 3.0 – Challenge 2025. This initiative focuses on the beautification and treatment of the wastewater channel near Annapurna Mess, with the goal of transforming it into a cleaner and greener space by May 30, 2025.

 
Défi ECO 2.0 (2023) – Best Out of Waste Competition
The Eco Club, ITM Gwalior successfully organized Défi ECO 2.0, a creative sustainability initiative focused on "Best out of Waste." Themed "Recycle It All, No Matter How Small," the activity encouraged students to craft innovative models using discarded household materials.
Participants worked in teams of two, creating models from items like plastic bottles, caps, newspapers, old magazines, fabric scraps, and other waste materials. The models were prepared at home and demonstrated on December 29, 2023
The event highlighted the importance of recycling and reusing everyday waste, fostering creativity and environmental awareness. The top three models were awarded certificates based on innovation, sustainability, and design.
Through this initiative, students explored practical sustainability solutions while engaging in a fun and educational activity.

 
Défi ECO 1.0 (2022) – An Engaging Environmental Challenge by Eco Club
The Eco Club at ITM Gwalior successfully organized an in-house activity, Défi ECO, which translates to Eco Challenge (Défi = Challenge in French). The event, themed "In Search of Our Ignored Surroundings," aimed to promote environmental awareness through interactive field activities.
A total of 60 students participated, forming three groups to tackle a series of three time-bound Eco Challenges. Each challenge tested their environmental knowledge, problem-solving skills, and teamwork. The best performers were awarded certificates for their outstanding efforts
This initiative provided students with an opportunity to engage with sustainability issues while fostering a sense of responsibility towards the environment.

RED RIBBON CLUB(RRC)
Red Ribbon Club is a movement started by the Government of India in schools and colleges through which, students spread awareness over HIV / AIDS. The red ribbon is a symbol for both drug prevention and  the fight against AIDS.
About the Red Ribbon Club (RRC)
Supported by the Madhya Pradesh State AIDS Control Society, NACO, and the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, the Red Ribbon Club at ITM Gwalior engages students in making meaningful contributions to social health issues. The club fosters leadership, community service, and awareness in a collaborative environment.
OBJECTIVES

HIV/AIDS Awareness: Educating the community about HIV/AIDS prevention, transmission, and reducing stigma.  
Voluntary Blood Donation: Promoting regular blood donation and the importance of safe blood donation practices. 
Life Skills Development: Raising awareness on responsible behavior, personal hygiene, and mental wellness. 
Youth Empowerment: Encouraging youth to take part in leadership and social responsibility activities. 

Key Activities & Initiatives

HIV/AIDS Awareness Campaigns: Street plays, poster-making, seminars, and rallies aimed at educating students and the local community.  
Blood Donation Camps: Organizing voluntary blood donation drives in collaboration with medical institutions. 
Workshops & Guest Lectures: Hosting talks on health, wellness, and disease prevention.
Health Check-up Camps: Providing medical check-ups for students and staff. 
Community Outreach Programs: Spreading awareness in nearby communities through various health initiatives. 

Impact & Achievements

Blood Donation Camps 
World AIDS Day Programs 
Recognition

GOOGLE DEVELOPER STUDENT CLUB (GDSC):
GDSC envisions empowering students through collaboration, innovation, and impactful solutions, fostering future tech leaders equipped to solve real-world challenges with cutting-edge technologies.
The Google Developer Student Clubs (GDSC) on Campus at ITM Gwalior is a vibrant, community-driven initiative designed to empower students with essential technical knowledge, hands-on experience, and opportunities to build impactful projects. This initiative fosters a culture of innovation and collaboration through a variety of activities, including workshops, hackathons, and community events. By providing a platform for students to learn and grow, GDSC has become a cornerstone of technical engagement at ITM Gwalior.
Vision and Mission
GDSC at ITM Gwalior was initiated in 2022 with a vision to create an inclusive community where students from diverse backgrounds could explore technology. The mission has always been to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical application, enabling students to become industry-ready professionals. By emphasizing collaboration, creativity, and continuous learning, GDSC aims to inspire students to address real-world challenges through technology.
Growth Since Inception
When GDSC was founded in 2022, it began with an enthusiastic group of 10 members. Through consistent efforts and a clear vision, the community grew to 25 members by 2025. This growth reflects the leadership team’s dedication to fostering a thriving ecosystem for technological innovation. Till now more than 700 students have participated in different events conducted by GDSC.
The club initially focused on building awareness about technology through interactive sessions, coding bootcamps, and workshops.
Key Activities and Achievements

Workshops and Training Sessions  
Hackathons 
Community Events
Collaborations with External Organizations 
Mentorship Programs 

Impact
The growth from 300 to 700 members in three years highlights GDSC’s impact. The club has enhanced members’ technical proficiency, confidence, and leadership skills. Many alumni have secured prestigious positions in leading tech companies, crediting their success to GDSC’s initiatives.
Beyond campus, GDSC’s online workshops and webinars have inspired students from other institutions, solidifying its reputation as a hub for technological excellence.
Plans for the Future
Advanced Training Programs  
The club plans to introduce programs in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Blockchain, and Cybersecurity, equipping students with cutting-edge skills.
Hybrid Events  
By organizing both online and offline events, GDSC ensures wider accessibility and participation.
Strengthening Partnerships  
The club aims to deepen collaborations with tech companies for internships, live projects, and knowledge-sharing sessions.
Interdisciplinary Projects
GDSC encourages projects involving students from different fields, broadening innovation and preparing members for diverse teams.
Promoting Inclusivity  
Inclusivity remains a core value. Initiatives will encourage participation from underrepresented groups, ensuring everyone feels welcome.
Conclusion
Since its inception in 2022, GDSC at ITM Gwalior has empowered students with technical knowledge and practical experience. As it enters its third year, the club is set to achieve greater milestones, inspiring tech enthusiasts to dream big and make a difference. GDSC continues to set an example for student communities, promising even more opportunities for growth and impact.

Event Details: TeckTrek’24 - Ideathon

DataVibe Network succesfully hosted its second event ‘Ideathon’ of TechTrek’24 with the aim to foster creativity among participants. This ideathon served as a platform for students to showcase their innovative ideas, tackle real-world challenges, and present their solutions through engaging presentations.
The teams for this event, each comprising of at most two students, were registered through a google form circulated among first-, second-, and third-year students of all branches. Each team was given an independent time-slot through emails to come up and present their ideas. The presentations were judged by the President and the Vice-President of DataVibe Network, and the winner and the runner-up were decided through the teams’ overall markings.
This event gave complete freedom to the participants in terms of ideas’ themes and topics. Hence, a diverse group of students participated, bringing forth unique ideas spanning various domains such as crime, healthcare, and education.
After around three-hours, the ideathon ended at 1:15 pm. The winner and the runner-up teams for the competition were informed and rewarded with achievement certificates, along with all the participants receiving a participation e-certificate through emails. 









Purpose of the Event:
1. Idea Diversity: The ideathon witnessed innovated ideas such as Road Accidents Sensor, Fake Profile Detector, and AI-powered mental assistant.
2. Engaging Presentations: Participants demonstrated impressive presentation skills, using visual and storytelling to communicate their ideas effectively.
Winners:
Winner team: ‘Debug Deo’ for their project idea on ‘WE (Work and Earn)’
Runner-up team: ‘The Life Survivors Team’ for their project idea on ‘Road Accident Sensor’.

Event Details: TeckTrek’24 - Hackathon

DataVibe Network conducted an exciting and high-energy Hackathon as its third and last event of its TechTrek’24 series, bringing together talented students to collaborate, innovate, and solve real-world challenges through coding and technology. The event provided a platform for participants to showcase their technical prowess and problem-solving skills under a competitive yet supportive environment.
The teams for this event were registered through a google form circulated among first-, second-, and third-year students of all branches. Each team consisted of no less three and no more than five members.
The themes for this event included Environment, Crime & Fraud, Education, Day to Day Life Easy Projects (Health, Finance, Groceries, etc.), or any relevant project for the futuristic purpose.
Teams were given four hours, starting from 11:00 am to 3:00 pm to develop their projects. A few teams started building their projects from scratch while others improvised their already existing ones, Throughout the competition, core team from the club provided guidance and technical support to ensure a productive and engaging experience. 
Participants were given a break of half an hour before their final presentation which ran from 3:30 pm to 4:50 pm. 









Purpose of the Event:
1. Innovative Projects: The Hackathon saw projects tackling disparate real-world challenges. These solutions showcased the participants’ creativity, technical expertise, and understanding of the themes.
2. Collaborative Spirit: Participants demonstrated excellent teamwork and problem-solving abilities as they worked under tight deadlines.
3. Engaging Demo: At the end of the hackathon, each team presented their project to a panel of judges, showcasing not just their technical implementations but also the real-world applicability of their solutions. 
Judging:
The judges for this event included President, Vice-President, and Technical Department Lead of the club, along with faculty coordinator Dr. Jitendra Singh Kushwah, Associate Professor of IT Department.
Winner team: ‘Code Crew’ for their project on ‘WE (Work and Earn)’
Runner-up team: ‘The Life Survivors Team’ for their project idea on ‘Road Accident Sensor’.

Electoral Literacy Club (ELC):
The Electoral Literacy Club (ELC) at ITM Gwalior is a student-driven initiative established to promote voter awareness, electoral participation, and democratic values among students. The club functions under the guidance of the Election Commission of India (ECI) aims to empower young voters by fostering electoral literacy and civic responsibility.
OBJECTIVES

To educate students about their electoral rights and responsibilities.
To encourage active participation in elections and democratic processes.
To organize awareness programs, workshops, and campaigns on voter registration.
To conduct activities such as mock elections, debates, quizzes, and street plays to engage students in electoral literacy.
To collaborate with government agencies and NGOs for voter awareness initiatives.

Key Activities & Initiatives

Voter Registration Drives: Assisting eligible students in registering as voters.  
Awareness Campaigns: Conducting street plays, poster-making competitions, and pledge ceremonies to promote electoral participation. 
Workshops & Seminars: Sessions by experts on topics like the importance of voting, EVM/VVPAT demonstrations, and electoral laws.
National Voter’s Day Celebrations: Organizing special events on January 25th to encourage student participation in the electoral process. 
Mock Elections: Simulating election processes to help students understand voting mechanisms. 

Impact & Achievements

Successfully registered and facilitated voter ID applications for hundreds of students. 
Organized multiple interactive sessions on the electoral process and voting rights. 
Received appreciation from local election authorities for outstanding voter awareness efforts.
Active participation in Election duties for local and assembly Election of Gwalior.
Active participation in university and district-level voter awareness programs.

Get Involved 
Students who are interested in contributing to electoral literacy and democratic engagement can join the ELC at ITM Gwalior. Together, we can build an informed and responsible voter community.
