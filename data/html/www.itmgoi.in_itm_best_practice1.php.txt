ITM Gwalior's Initiatives During COVID-19
Twenty-five students from the NSS unit of ITM College demonstrated remarkable dedication by serving at the Corona Vaccination Center at Jiwaji University Hospital, City Centre, Gwalior, for 20 consecutive days. Their commitment to community service during the Covid-19 pandemic reflects their willingness to step up and contribute to public health efforts during a critical time.
These students assisted with various tasks at the vaccination center, such as managing queues, guiding people through the vaccination process, helping with registration, and ensuring that Covid-19 protocols were followed. Their service not only supported the healthcare staff but also helped streamline the vaccination process, making it more efficient and accessible for the community.
This initiative by the NSS volunteers of ITM College highlights the significant role that young individuals can play in public health and crisis management. Their selfless service stands as an inspiring example of how volunteerism can make a tangible impact in times of need.

ITM unit helps the poor in withdrawing the financial assistance amount sent by the Government of India.
Mr Narendra Kumar Verma, Program Officer and Volunteers of NSS ITM Group of Institutions Gwalior, is giving his full contribution in serving the poor during the Covid-19 lockdown. While addressing the press, Mr Narendra Kumar said that he had received information that the assistance amount of Rs 1000, gas cylinder amount, and amount for wage card holders was sent by the Government of India for the help of the poor. To withdraw it, some people were taking advantage of their helplessness and charging Rs 50-100. Then NSS Unit decided that I will not let this system flourish, at least in my area. we contacted Mr Vinod Mama, Kiosk Operator SBI Tansen Nagar Gwalior, then with his help we started the work of withdrawing the full amount without any extra charge to all the needy people in Scindia Nagar for three days. Till now we have withdrawn the assistance of about 185 families without any extra charge and handed it over to them. We did not spend any money, we only have time for those needy people, which is our duty as a community volunteers. I request all those capable people that if such an incident is happening in your area, please make those needy people aware and inform them.

Unnat Bharat Abhiyan Cell ITM Gwalior
Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) is a flagship program initiated by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, with the vision to involve higher educational institutions (HEIs) in the development of rural India. For institutions seeking NAAC (National Assessment and Accreditation Council) accreditation, implementing UBA can serve as an excellent best practice program. Here's how Unnat Bharat Abhiyan aligns with NAAC's criteria and can be showcased effectively:
Alignment with NAAC Criteria:
UBA aligns well with several NAAC criteria, such as:
												● Criterion III: Research, Innovations, and Extension: UBA projects focus on the extension activities and societal outreach. Institutions can demonstrate their commitment to rural development, technology transfer, and social innovation through UBA.
												● Criterion V: Student Support and Progression: By involving students in UBA activities, institutions can show how they provide opportunities for experiential learning, community engagement, and leadership development.
												● Criterion VII: Institutional Values and Best Practices: UBA reflects institutional values like social responsibility, sustainability, and inclusivity. It demonstrates the institution's role in contributing to national development priorities.
UBA Activities as Best Practice:
To showcase UBA as a best practice, the following points should be highlighted:
												● Selection of Villages: Describe the process of selecting villages for intervention and the baseline survey conducted to understand local needs.
												● Engagement and Projects: Detail the specific projects undertaken, such as sanitation drives, renewable energy initiatives, water conservation, digital literacy programs, agricultural innovations, etc.
												● Community Participation: Emphasize the participatory approach involving local communities, Panchayats, and other stakeholders, ensuring that the initiatives are demand-driven and sustainable.
												● Student Involvement: Highlight the active involvement of students and faculty in planning and implementing projects, including internships, research, and fieldwork.
												● Outcomes and Impact: Provide measurable outcomes like the number of beneficiaries, improvements in quality of life, enhanced awareness, and specific success stories.
The Unnat Bharat Abhiyan (UBA) Cell of ITM Gwalior has been instrumental in driving development projects in its adopted villages by formally communicating the needs and requirements to the District Collector. Through these written communications, the UBA ITM Cell effectively highlighted the areas requiring attention, prompting action from local governing bodies such as the Janpad Panchayat and Jila Panchayat.
As a result of these efforts, significant work has been accomplished in the adopted villages of Badera and Sarnagat. One key project initiated through UBA ITM’s advocacy is the construction of boundaries around government schools in these villages.This initiative not only helps secure the school premises but also creates a more conducive environment for education, reflecting the direct impact of UBA’s involvement.
The collaboration between UBA ITM Cell and local government bodies illustrates the power of strategic communication and advocacy in community development. By acting as a bridge between the villages and government authorities, UBA ITM has played a crucial role in ensuring that essential infrastructural needs are met, demonstrating the effectiveness of coordinated efforts in rural development.

As part of the Unnat Bharat Abhiyan initiative, ITM University’s UBA Unit organized a comprehensive survey program aimed at identifying critical issues in local villages. Through this program, volunteers engaged with community members to gather data on various socio-economic and infrastructural challenges faced by the rural population. The findings from this survey were compiled into a detailed report, which was subsequently submitted to the district collector for necessary action and intervention.
The survey focused on areas such as education, health, sanitation, water resources, and livelihood opportunities. By identifying these key areas for development, ITM’s UBA Unit demonstrated its commitment to bridging the gap between academic institutions and rural development. The report highlights the critical needs of the community, serving as a foundation for targeted government action and resource allocation.
This initiative reflects ITM’s proactive role in contributing to sustainable rural development and aligns with the broader objectives of the Unnat Bharat Abhiyan, which seeks to improve the quality of life in India’s villages through collaborative efforts between educational institutions and government bodies.

ITM College Partnership with My Bharat Portal
ITM College has taken a significant step toward fostering national service and community engagement by registering as a partner institution on the My Bharat portal. This platform, initiated by the Government of India, aims to connect youth with various national programs and opportunities for social contribution. Through this partnership, more than 500 ITM students have already registered on the portal, demonstrating their commitment to civic duty and leadership.
The My Bharat portal provides a streamlined way for students to participate in activities such as volunteering, community service, and youth development initiatives. ITM College’s active promotion of this platform encourages students to enhance their personal and professional growth through hands-on experiences that contribute to society. By engaging students in activities beyond the classroom, this practice not only supports their overall development but also aligns with the broader goals of nation-building and responsible citizenship.
